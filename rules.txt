# Set / Rank
Only cards with an even rank (2,4,6,8,10,12).
Only face cards (11,12,13).
Only cards between 1 and 7 inclusive.
Only cards between 5 and 9 inclusive.
Only ranks that are prime numbers (2,3,5,7,11,13).
Only Aces (rank 1) .

# Set / Suit
Only cards of the suit spades.
Only red cards (hearts or diamonds).
Only spades and diamonds.
Only hearts, clubs, and diamonds allowed. Spades are forbidden.

# Set / Both
Only black face cards.
Only red cards whose rank is <=7.
Face cards (11-13) must be red; number cards (1-10) must be black.

# Rank alternation or evolution
Alternate face and number cards. Any card may start the line.
Card rank must have opposite odd/even parity to the previous card's rank. Any card may start the line.
Each card must have a rank greater or equal to the previous card. Only Ace can start the line.

# Suit alternation or evolution
Cards must alternate between red and black colors. Any card may start the line.
The card must be of a different suit than the card just before it. Any card may start the line.
The card must be of a different suit than but same color as the card just before it. Any card may start the line.
Suits must repeat in the cyclic order hearts → spades → clubs → diamonds → hearts... Any card may start the line.
Hearts and spades form Group A; clubs and diamonds form Group B. Alternate between groups. Any card may start the line.

# Complex alternation
Each card must share at least one property with the previous card: same color, or same parity. Any card may start the line.
If the previous card was red, rank must increase or be equal; if black, rank must decrease or be equal. Starting card must be between 5 and 9 inclusive.
Face cards imposes the suit: if a face card is played, the next card must match its suit. Otherwise, the next card must be a different suit than it.

# Pairs of properties
Suits must appear in pairs: card 1 and 2 same suit, cards 3 and 4 same suit (different from 1 and 2), cards 5 and 6 same suit (different from 3 and 4), etc.
Rank repeats in pairs: ranks must come in doubles: (x, x), then (y, y) with y different from x, then (z, z) with z different from y, etc.
