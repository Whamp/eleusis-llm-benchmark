# Rank constraints
Only cards with an even rank (2,4,6,8,10,12) may be played.
Only cards with an odd rank (1,3,5,7,9,11,13) may be played.
Only face cards (11,12,13) may be played.
Only number cards (1-10) may be played.
Only ranks that are multiples of three (3,6,9,12) are allowed.
Only cards whose rank is between 5 and 9 inclusive may be played.
Only Aces (rank 1) or Kings (rank 13) may be placed.
Only Aces (rank 1) may be placed.
Only ranks that are perfect squares (1, 4, 9) may be played.
Only prime number ranks (2,3,5,7,11,13) may be played.
Only ranks that are multiples of 4 or 5 (4, 5, 8, 10, 12) may be played.

# Rank alternation
Card rank must have opposite odd/even parity to the previous card's rank.
Alternate face and number cards.
If the previous rank was <7, the new card must be >7. If the previous rank was >7, the new card must be <7. 7 is forbidden.
No two consecutive cards may have the same rank.
Digit Count: You must alternate between single-digit ranks (A–9) and double-digit ranks.
Rank repeats in pairs: ranks must come in doubles: (x, x), then a different rank (y, y), then different (z, z)…
Parity repeats in pairs : two odd ranks, then two even ranks, then two odd ranks, etc.

# Increase / Decrease based
Each card must have a rank greater than or equal to the previous card; any card may start the line. No cycle allowed.
Each card must have a rank less than or equal to the previous card; any card may start the line. No cycle allowed.
Each card must have a rank strictly greater than the previous card; any card may start the line. Ace may follow King.
Each card must have a rank strictly less than the previous card; any card may start the line. King may follow Ace.
The absolute difference between this card’s rank and the previous card’s rank must be less than or equal to 2.
The absolute difference between this card’s rank and the previous card’s rank must be exactly 1. Ace may follow King and vice versa.
Each pair of consecutive cards must have ranks that sum to more than 7.

# Suit based
Only cards of the suit spades may be played.
Major vs. Minor: Only cards from the "Major Suits" (Hearts and Spades) may be played.
Only spades and diamonds may be played.

# Color based
Every card must be red (hearts or diamonds).
Every card must be black (spades or clubs).

# Suit alternation
The card must be of a different suit than the card just before it. Any card may start the line.
The card must be of a different suit than but same color as the card just before it. Any card may start the line.
Suits must repeat in the order hearts → spades → clubs → diamonds → hearts …
Suits repeat every three cards in the order hearts → clubs → spades → hearts … (diamonds never appear).
Suits must appear in pairs: card 1 and 2 same suit, cards 3 and 4 same suit (different from 1 and 2), cards 5 and 6 same suit (different from 3 and 4), etc.
The suit changes every three cards; the first three may be any suit, the next three must be a different suit, and so on.
The card’s suit must differ from each of the previous two suits (if they exist).
Hearts and spades form Group A; clubs and diamonds form Group B. Alternate between groups.

# Color alternation
Cards must alternate between red and black colors.
Colors must be the same in triplets: the first three cards must be the same color, the next three must be the opposite color, and so on.
No more than two consecutive cards may be the same color.

# Mix rank and suit/color
If the previous rank is even, the suit must match the previous suit; if odd, the suit must differ.
If the previous rank is even, the new card must keep the same color; if odd, the color must switch.
Only red cards whose rank is prime numbers (2,3,5,7,11,13) may be played.
Each card must share at least one property with the previous card: same color, or same parity.
If the previous card was red, rank must increase; if black, rank must decrease. Ace may follow King and vice versa.
Face cards must be red; number cards (1-10) must be black.
Face cards imposes the suit: if a face card is played, the next card must match its suit. Otherwise, the next card must be a different suit than it.
No attribute match rule: between consecutive cards, none of {color, parity, face-vs-number} may match.
The High/Low Switch: If the previous card was Red, the played card must be High (8–K). If the previous card was Black, the played card must be Low (A–7).
